High-fidelity website UI concept mockup, desktop 16:9, for a software engineer's personal portfolio. Use the attached reference (ref.png) for the page's layout only: the same structure (a slim masthead strip across the top, a hero row of two panels, the left one a large headline and intro, the right one a product screenshot in a mounted screen with two buttons under it, then a row of three smaller project cards, each with a small screen). Do not copy its style.

Art direction "Natural Garden": a sunlit cottage garden painted as a botanical watercolour. Behind everything, a loose, luminous watercolour of an English cottage garden in late-morning sun: tall hollyhocks, foxgloves and delphiniums framing the left edge, a climbing rose over a weathered wooden arch and a terracotta pot of lavender at the right, a mossy flagstone path and low clipped box along the bottom, soft sky and a hazy hedge in the far distance, the centre a calm stretch of pale sunlit wash.

The content panels are botanical plates: sheets of warm cream hot-pressed watercolour paper with a softly deckled edge, each with a small, finely painted watercolour spray of garden flowers and leaves tucked into its top-left and bottom-right corners only, the rest of the sheet plain, with a hair-thin sage-green ruled border just inside the edge, like a botanical print. Buttons are small plaques of pale honey limestone set flush, with a softly chiselled bevel and a fine sandy texture; the primary button is a glazed terracotta tile. Screens are set into a thin moss-green card mat. Status markers are tiny painted flower heads (a white daisy, a green bud, a yellow buttercup, a pink rose). Labels are written on narrow strips of seed-packet kraft paper. Headings in a refined garden-catalogue serif.

Palette: cream paper #f6f0e2, sage green #8fa77f, moss #4f6b45, limestone #e4d8bd, terracotta #c46a43 (the one accent), rose #d98c9a, ink #2d3328. Warm late-morning sunlight from the upper left. Fresh, crafted, botanical, expensive; generous space; a AAA game menu, not a flat website. Placeholder text only as abstract lines except the large headline "Tools I use every day".
