Art direction "Natural Garden": a walled cottage garden with stone-set plaques, painted in botanical watercolour. Behind everything, a sunlit cottage garden in watercolour and fine ink line: an old honey-coloured dry-stone wall running across the bottom with climbing roses and clematis spilling over it, tall foxgloves and hollyhocks at the left, a weathered wooden gate and a fruit tree at the right, soft blue sky with a few clouds, the centre calm and pale.

The content panels are plaques of pale honey limestone set into the garden like tablets in a wall: smooth dressed stone with a chiselled bevelled border, faint fossil and grain in the stone, a few trailing sprigs of ivy over one top corner of each. Buttons are glazed ceramic tiles: a sage-green glaze for the neutral button, a terracotta glaze for the primary, each with a softly rounded edge and a little glaze pooling. Screens are set into a bronze-edged recess cut in the stone. Status markers are small glazed ceramic beads. Labels are on narrow strips of weathered copper plant-label. Headings in a refined classical serif.

Palette: limestone #e8dcc0, sage #8fa77f, moss #4f6b45, terracotta #c46a43 (the one accent), rose #d98c9a, ink #2d3328. Warm late-morning sunlight from the upper left.
