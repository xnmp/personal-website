High-fidelity website UI concept mockup, desktop 16:9, for a software engineer's personal portfolio. Use the attached reference (ref.png) for the page's layout only: the same structure (a slim masthead strip across the top, a hero row of two panels, the left one a large headline and intro, the right one a product screenshot in a mounted screen with two buttons under it, then a row of three smaller project cards, each with a small screen). Do not copy its style.

Art direction "Celestial Atlas": a celestial atlas in an old observatory. Behind everything, the interior of a night observatory: the dome's shutter open to a deep navy sky full of stars and faint gold-engraved constellation figures, a great brass refracting telescope at the right, an armillary sphere and a brass astrolabe on a table at the left, the centre open night sky.

The content panels are pages of an antique star atlas: aged ivory laid paper with a faint foxing, framed by a fine double rule of gold leaf and a narrow deep-navy border band printed with tiny engraved stars, the corners finished with small engraved compass-rose rosettes. Buttons are small engraved brass plates with a bevelled edge; the primary is a brass plate enamelled deep crimson. Screens are set in a ring of engraved brass like an instrument's limb, with fine degree ticks (no numbers). Status markers are small glass jewels set in brass. Labels are on strips of navy ribbon. Headings in an engraved copperplate serif.

Palette: navy #13213f, deep navy #0b1428, ivory #f1e8d2, gold leaf #c9a35a, brass #b8893f, crimson #9e2b2f (the one accent), ink #1b1a17. Warm lamplight from the upper left against the cool starlight. Scholarly, precious, expensive; Generous space; a AAA game menu, not a flat website.
