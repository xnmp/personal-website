Art direction "Celestial Atlas": brass astronomical instruments over a star chart. Behind everything, one enormous antique star chart printed in gold on deep navy: the constellations drawn as engraved figures and dotted lines, the Milky Way as a soft stipple, a great brass astrolabe partly visible at the left edge and an orrery at the right, the centre calm deep navy with faint stars.

The content panels are brass instrument frames: each a rectangle of deep navy enamel held in a frame of engraved brass with fine degree graduations along the frame (no numbers), small brass screws at the corners. Buttons are brass plates with an engraved border; the primary is a plate of crimson enamel in brass. Screens are set behind a brass bezel. Status markers are small glass jewels in brass settings. Labels are engraved on narrow brass strips. Headings in an engraved copperplate serif in gold.

Palette: navy #13213f, deep navy #0b1428, gold #c9a35a, brass #b8893f, ivory #f1e8d2, crimson #9e2b2f (the one accent). Warm lamplight from the upper left.
