Environment background art for a website, very wide. A calm, bright electronics workbench in soft daylight photographed from straight above: a large expanse of pale warm-grey linoleum desk surface with an extremely subtle fine texture, lit by soft diffuse window light from the upper-left with a gentle falloff to the lower-right. At the far edges only, out of focus: the corner of an off-white keyboard, a coiled vermilion cable, a few machined screws. The center 70% must be calm, empty and low-contrast so UI can sit on top. Restrained, photographic. No text, no logos, no people.
