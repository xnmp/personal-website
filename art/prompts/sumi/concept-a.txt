High-fidelity website UI concept mockup, desktop 16:9, for a software engineer's personal portfolio. Use the attached reference (ref.png) for the page's layout only: the same structure (a slim masthead strip across the top, a hero row of two panels, the left one a large headline and intro, the right one a product screenshot in a mounted screen with two buttons under it, then a row of three smaller project cards, each with a small screen). Do not copy its style.

Art direction "Sumi-e Ink": ink wash and watercolour on washi. Behind everything, a sumi-e landscape painted on warm, fibrous washi paper: layered misty mountains in graded grey ink washes receding into white mist, a gnarled pine on a rock at the far left, a bamboo grove and a small thatched pavilion at the far right, a few pale watercolour washes of indigo and ochre, a flight of three birds; the centre is calm mist and empty paper.

The content panels are hanging-scroll mounts: a panel of cream kozo washi with long visible fibres, framed by a narrow border of plain pale celadon silk and a hair-thin gold paper line, like a kakejiku mount. Buttons are small slips of thick cream washi card with a thin sumi brush border; the primary button is a vermilion seal-stamp block, its edges slightly broken like a real stamp impression. Screens are mounted in deep indigo silk borders with a thin gold inner line. Status markers are small round seal impressions (vermilion, jade, ochre, black). Labels are on narrow strips of indigo-dyed washi. Headings set in an elegant high-contrast serif; a single vermilion seal stamp sits beside the brand.

Palette: warm washi #f3ecdc, sumi ink #1d1d1f, ink greys, pale celadon #c9d3c0, indigo #2f3e5c, vermilion #c8371e (the one accent), ochre #c49a5a. Soft daylight from the upper left. Restrained, quiet, expensive; generous empty space; a AAA game menu, not a flat website. Placeholder text only as abstract lines except the large headline "Tools I use every day".
