High-fidelity website UI concept mockup, desktop 16:9, for a software engineer's personal portfolio. Use the attached reference (ref.png) for the page's layout only: the same structure (a slim masthead strip across the top, a hero row of two panels, the left one a large headline and intro, the right one a product screenshot in a mounted screen with two buttons under it, then a row of three smaller project cards, each with a small screen). Do not copy its style.

Art direction "Sumi-e Ink": ink wash and watercolour on washi. Behind everything, a wide ink-wash landscape: towering misty peaks in layered grey and blue-grey washes, waterfalls, pines clinging to cliffs at the left and right edges, a fisherman's boat on a still lake along the bottom, everything dissolving into white mist in the centre, painted on warm washi with visible fibres.

The content panels are sheets of thin, slightly translucent cream washi with soft feathered fibrous edges, laid flat and held by small strips of indigo washi tape at the top. Each panel has a single large sumi brush stroke as a rule under its heading. Buttons are cream washi card tabs with a dry-brush ink outline; the primary button is solid vermilion with a brushed edge. Screens are mounted on dark ink-black paper with a feathered edge. Status markers are small round vermilion and ink seal dots. Headings in an elegant high-contrast serif; a vermilion seal stamp beside the brand.

Palette: warm washi #f2ead8, sumi ink #1c1c1e, ink greys, blue-grey wash #8a97a6, vermilion #c8371e (the one accent), ochre #c49a5a. Soft daylight from the upper left. Restrained, quiet, expensive; generous empty space; a AAA game menu, not a flat website. Placeholder text only as abstract lines except the large headline "Tools I use every day".
