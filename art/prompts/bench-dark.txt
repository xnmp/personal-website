Environment background art for a website, very wide. A dim, quiet electronics workbench at night photographed from straight above: a large expanse of dark graphite anti-static mat with an extremely subtle fine grid texture, softly lit by a single warm desk lamp pool from the upper-left that falls off into near-black at the right and bottom. At the far edges only, out of focus: the corner of a keyboard, a coiled vermilion cable, a few machined screws. The center 70% must be calm, empty and low-contrast so UI can sit on top. Cinematic, restrained, photographic, shallow depth of field. No text, no logos, no people.

Post-processing (current install): the authored desk carried props (lamp, mug,
