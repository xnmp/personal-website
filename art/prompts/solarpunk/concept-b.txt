High-fidelity website UI concept mockup, desktop 16:9, for a software engineer's personal portfolio. Use the attached reference (ref.png) for the page's layout only: the same structure (a slim masthead strip across the top, a hero row of two panels, the left one a large headline and intro, the right one a product screenshot in a mounted screen with two buttons under it, then a row of three smaller project cards, each with a small screen). Do not copy its style.

Art direction "Solarpunk": a golden-hour greenhouse workstation. Behind everything, a painted solarpunk conservatory at golden hour: tall curved glass walls with fine brass mullions, terraced planters spilling ferns and flowering vines down the far left and right edges, a few hanging brass lanterns, moss and small succulents along a stone sill at the bottom, the warm low sun pouring in from the upper left through the glass. The centre of the backdrop is calm warm haze and distant glass.

The content panels are cream vitreous-enamel plaques with softly rounded corners, set in a thin bevelled brass rim, slightly glossy where the warm light catches the top-left. Buttons are small enamel-and-brass push plates: moss-green enamel, the primary one marigold enamel, each in a brass bezel with a bevel. Screens are set into deep brass-and-walnut bezels. Status markers are round enamel indicator lamps in brass cups. Labels are on engraved brass tags.

Palette: honey-gold light, brass #b48a3f, verdigris #5b9585, moss #56703f, cream enamel #f1e8d2, marigold #e5a03a, ink #20291f. Serif headline, clean sans body. Lighting soft and warm from the upper left, consistent on every element. Expensive, handmade, calm; a AAA game menu, not a flat website.
