A sheet of four separate bunches of ivy on a fully transparent background (real alpha, no backdrop), arranged in a 2x2 grid with wide empty gaps between them so that no bunch touches another or the edge of the canvas. Painted like the ivy that spills over the bottom of the brass picture frames in the reference image (ref.png): heart-shaped (cordate) leaves, rounded lobes and a short pointed tip, on thin wiry stems, in a rich mid-green and deep green with warm yellow-green where the low golden sun catches their edges, a natural satin sheen as of sunlit leaves in a painted photograph, veins faint. The leaves are LARGE: the biggest are about one tenth of the canvas width, the smaller ones about half that.
Each bunch is ivy draped over an invisible horizontal bar across the middle of its quadrant, as ivy spills over the bottom rail of a frame: a thick cluster of nine to fourteen overlapping heart-shaped leaves of mixed sizes growing from a point on the bar, some leaves above the bar standing up and turned toward the viewer, most hanging down below it and splaying outward, a few leaves on thin stems trailing left or right along the bar; one or two tendrils curl. The bunch is wider than tall. The four bunches clearly differ from each other: the first is a dense cluster with a longer trail to the left, the second a smaller pair of large leaves with a few tiny ones, the third a big spreading bunch with a trail to the right, the fourth an even cluster with a long thin vine to each side.
NO specular highlights, NO sparkle, NO glints, NO white highlight dots, NO dew, NO wet glossy blobs. No frame, no wall, no floor, no background, no cast shadow: only the ivy on transparency. Square canvas, at the highest resolution available.
