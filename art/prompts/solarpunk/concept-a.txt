High-fidelity website UI concept mockup, desktop 16:9, for a software engineer's personal portfolio. Use the attached reference (ref.png) for the page's layout only: the same structure (a slim masthead strip across the top, a hero row of two panels, the left one a large headline and intro, the right one a product screenshot in a mounted screen with two buttons under it, then a row of three smaller project cards, each with a small screen). Do not copy its style.

Art direction "Solarpunk": a golden-hour greenhouse workstation. Behind everything, a painted Victorian glasshouse interior at golden hour: slender white-and-verdigris iron glazing bars and arched panes overhead, warm low sun streaming in from the upper left, ferns, monstera, trailing pothos and hanging baskets of plants at the far left and right edges, moss on old brick and a potting bench along the bottom, dust motes in the light. The centre of the backdrop is calm, softly lit glass and haze.

The content panels are panes of frosted, slightly green-tinted glass held in thin polished brass frames with small round brass rivets at the corners, catching the warm light along their top-left edges; a sprig of moss or a tiny fern tucked into one corner of some frames only. Buttons are enamel-and-brass: cream or deep moss-green enamel plates set in a brass bezel, like old instrument controls; the primary button is a warm marigold enamel. Screens are mounted in brass-framed recesses. Status markers are small enamel-and-brass indicator studs. Labels are on small brass name-plates.

Palette: warm honey light, brass #b8893b, verdigris #5f9c8a, moss green #4f6b3a, cream enamel #efe6cf, marigold #e8a33c, deep bottle green ink #1f2b22. Serif headline, clean sans body. Lighting soft and warm from the upper left, consistent on every element. Expensive, handmade, calm; a AAA game menu, not a flat website.
