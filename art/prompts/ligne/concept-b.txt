High-fidelity website UI concept mockup, desktop 16:9, for a software engineer's personal portfolio. Use the attached reference (ref.png) for the page's layout only: the same structure (a slim masthead strip across the top, a hero row of two panels, the left one a large headline and intro, the right one a product screenshot in a mounted screen with two buttons under it, then a row of three smaller project cards, each with a small screen). Do not copy its style.

Art direction "Ligne Claire": the bridge of a 1960s retro-futurist exploration ship drawn in the European clear-line comic style: one even weight of black ink line, flat pastel colours, no hatching. Behind everything, through a wide curved viewport, a desert planet: banded pastel mesas, a pale ringed planet low in a peach sky, twin small moons, the centre calm sky.

The content panels are the ship's console plates: flat pastel-enamelled panels (mint and cream) with rounded corners, each edged with an even ink line and a second inset line, a row of drawn rivet dots along the top. Buttons are flat pill-shaped toggle switches in pastel enamel with an ink outline; the primary is coral-red. Screens are set in a thick rounded ink bezel. Status markers are round indicator lamps. Labels are small enamel tags. Headings in a clean, slightly condensed comic-lettering sans.

Palette: cream #fbf3e1, mint #cfe8d5, powder blue #bcd7ea, apricot #f2c39b, coral #e2553f (the one accent), ink #1c1c1c. Even light from the upper left. Clean, optimistic, graphic, expensive; Generous space; a AAA game menu, not a flat website. Placeholder text only as abstract lines except the large headline "Tools I use every day".
