High-fidelity website UI concept mockup, desktop 16:9, for a software engineer's personal portfolio. Use the attached reference (ref.png) for the page's layout only: the same structure (a slim masthead strip across the top, a hero row of two panels, the left one a large headline and intro, the right one a product screenshot in a mounted screen with two buttons under it, then a row of three smaller project cards, each with a small screen). Do not copy its style.

Art direction "Ligne Claire": a page from a European science-fiction comic album drawn in the clear-line style: every outline one even weight of black ink, no hatching, flat pastel colour fields, crisp cast shadows as flat shapes. Behind everything, a wide desert establishing shot: pale apricot dunes and mesas under a sky of flat pastel bands (peach to powder blue), a domed research outpost and a parked retro-futurist rocket at the right, rock arches and a lone antenna mast at the left, the centre open sky and sand.

The content panels are comic panels: flat pastel colour fields (cream, pale mint) inside a single even black ink border with slightly rounded corners, set apart by white gutters like a comic page, each with a flat drop shadow offset to the lower right. Buttons are caption boxes: flat pale-yellow rectangles with an even ink border; the primary is a flat coral-red caption box. Screens are framed as an inset panel with a thick ink border. Status markers are small flat round lamps drawn in ink with a flat colour fill. Labels are speech-tag captions with a small pointer. Headings in a clean, slightly condensed comic-lettering sans.

Palette: cream #fbf3e1, mint #cfe8d5, powder blue #bcd7ea, apricot #f2c39b, pale yellow #fbe7a1, coral #e2553f (the one accent), ink #1c1c1c. Even daylight from the upper left, shadows as flat shapes. Crisp, optimistic, graphic, expensive; Generous space; a AAA game menu, not a flat website.
