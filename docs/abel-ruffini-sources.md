# Abel–Ruffini material

The overview and worked chapters are edited from the owner's
`Documents/polynomial-solvability` notes and their associated Claude Code
conversation of 26 October 2026. The conversation's motivating questions —
why symmetry predicts a radical, which direction the recursion runs, and
what is new about two commuting swaps — determine the reading order.
The original files and private conversation are not modified or published.

These are shorter adaptations, not transcript dumps. They distinguish generic
coefficients from specialisations, require the roots of unity used as fixed
scalars, coordinate radical branches, and separate the elementary construction
from the full solvability theorem. The original quartic's four-cycle detour is
summarised without its incorrect intermediate “known sum” claim.

The formal criterion is checked against J. S. Milne's
[*Fields and Galois Theory*](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/FT0.pdf),
Theorem 3.28 and Section 5. Source changes go in
`content/projects/abel-ruffini.md` and `content/abel-ruffini-notes.md`.
