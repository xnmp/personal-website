<!-- block: headline -->
A diary I can keep as files

<!-- block: introduction -->
I used Daylio, and I liked the basic workflow of picking a mood, ticking a few activities, and writing whatever I felt like. 

But to get the entries to a place where it could be processed by an LLM, I'd have to manually create a backup and put it on Google drive. 

I wanted to streamline this process, make it Syncable to my computer with Syncthing, and in doing so I added a dream catching functionality which until then I'd been using a separate app for, and just make it look nicer.
